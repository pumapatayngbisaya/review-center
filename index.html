About GeraBananini

🍌 What is GeraBananini?

GeraBananini Review Station is your offline-friendly, browser-based study companion. Upload a text document, get a concise review, and challenge yourself with an auto-generated quiz — all without sending your data anywhere.

🛠️ How it generates quizzes

The app reads your document, extracts key sentences and concepts, and builds multiple-choice questions on the fly using in-browser JavaScript. No AI API calls needed — everything runs locally in your browser.

🔒 Privacy

Your files never leave your device. All processing happens in the browser. Session history is saved only to your browser's local storage.
